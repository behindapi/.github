## What is behindapi?

**behindapi** is an engineering space for exploring what makes software work beneath the surface.
We build across backend and full-stack systems, AI, and data science — with an emphasis on understanding the fundamentals, solving real problems, and keeping the implementation as simple as the problem allows.
The goal isn't to collect technologies. It's to build useful things, understand the trade-offs, and share what we learn along the way.

## The approach

| ◈ | Principle | In practice |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| ⚙ | **End to end** | Complete projects, from the first useful feature to a deployable product. |
| ⌁ | **Ready to use** | Solve a real need with a clear, approachable experience. |
| ↗ | **Built to grow** | Leave room for sustainable products and subscription plans. |
| ⌘ | **Keep it simple** | Choose the straightforward solution and let the software do its work. |
